# 🎓 Student Life Manager

Student Life Manager is a website that helps students organize their university life in one place. You can manage your class schedule, track your income and expenses, plan your study sessions, and calculate your GPA.

**For example:**
- 📅 Add your class schedule and assignment deadlines so you don't forget important tasks.
- 💰 Record your daily expenses, such as lunch, transportation, and shopping, to see where your money goes.
- 📚 Plan your study time before exams and keep track of what you need to review.
- 🎯 Calculate your GPA and find out what grades you need to reach your target GPA.

The goal is to make student life easier, help you manage your time and money, and stay on track with your academic goals.
